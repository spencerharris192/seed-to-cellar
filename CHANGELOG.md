# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-02): first release

For Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.

### Farming
- 30 crops and 8 fruit trees, each with its own way of growing: row crops, two-block corn, paddy rice, trellis vines (hops,
  red and white grapes), berry bushes, cut-and-regrow herbs, desert agave and vanilla on jungle logs.
- Wild crops in their own biomes, wild fruit trees and orchards; barley, oat and rye seeds from grass.
- Climates (Cold, Temperate, Warm, Hot) and greenhouses; compost, the Compost Bin and Fertile Farmland; sickles; straw,
  thatch, bales, sacks and crates; the Drying Rack.

### Kitchen
- Flours, doughs and breads; kettle stews, soups and jams; jar pickles, sauerkraut and kimchi; placeable pies, Black
  Forest Cake and the Harvest Feast; olive oil dishes; Farmer's Delight Cooking Pot and Cutting Board recipes.

### Brewing, winemaking and distilling
- Malting Tub, Kiln, Millstone, Brew Kettle, Fermenting Vat, Preserving Jar, Crushing Tub, Fruit Press and Pot Still.
- 8 beers; red, white and rosé wine, cider, perry, mead, 10 fruit wines and sake; whiskey, bourbon, vodka, brandy, 5 fruit
  brandies, rum, tequila, grappa and gin; 8 liqueurs, bitters, spiced rum and Apple Crown Whiskey; juices, lemonade,
  cordial, coffee and ginger beer.
- Wild and cultured yeasts (ale, lager, wine), fermentation temperature from where the vat stands, quality stars with a
  checklist on every drink, and a secret sixth star.
- Casks in all ten vanilla woods, ideal woods per drink, charred casks, solera blending, kegs; spirits that change name and
  color with age.

### Tavern and decor
- Wine Rack, Bottle Shelf and Wine Display showing your real bottles in 3D; drinks set down on any surface; bar counters
  with corners, bar stools to sit on, a mug rack, hop garlands, hanging bundles, a tavern sign; Brew Kettle and Pot Still
  weather like copper.

### World and people
- The Vintner and the Brewer, with Vineyards and Brewhouses (and their cellars) in villages; wandering trader seeds and
  saplings; finds in vanilla chests (seeds, agave, vanilla, aged rum, old wine and spirits).
- Advancements through farming, brewing, winemaking, distilling and a few just for fun.
- Drink effects (Refreshed, Warmth, Courage and a few vanilla ones), tipsiness with accessible view sway, hangovers and
  hiccups, all configurable.

### Guidance and integrations
- A "what is this / next" line on every item; the Hydrometer; The Brewer's Almanac (Patchouli).
- JEI pages for every station and plant, with every item and liquid showing how to get it; Jade and The One Probe
  read-outs.
- Create, Mekanism, Immersive Engineering, Botany Pots, Serene Seasons, Tough As Nails and Cold Sweat support; recipe
  formats for KubeJS and CraftTweaker in `docs/PACK_MAKERS.md`.
- Tested so none of our recipes competes with Farmer's Delight's, Quark's, Supplementaries', Create's, Mekanism's or
  Immersive Engineering's for the same input.
